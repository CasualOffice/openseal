Agreements, signed and provable.

OpenSeal is an open-source electronic-signature platform: upload a PDF, place fields, send, and get back a sealed document with its audit trail that anyone can verify offline. Self-hostable, embeddable and API-first.

Open the app Verify a signed PDF

Pre-release. Designed to support e-signature laws such as ESIGN and eIDAS; no legal or compliance claim is made.

What OpenSeal does

  • Send and track

    Upload documents, add recipients in signing order, place fields, and follow every envelope from sent to completed.

  • Sign anywhere

    Recipients sign in the browser on a phone or a desktop, with no account and no download, guided field by field.

  • Verify offline

    Each completed document is sealed with its audit trail inside, so anyone can check who signed and that nothing changed.

  • Self-host and API

    Run it on your own servers, embed signing in your product, and drive everything through the same API the app uses.

OpenSeal is open source under the Apache License 2.0.
