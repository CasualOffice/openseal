Agreements, signed and provable.

OpenSeal is an open-source electronic-signature platform under Apache-2.0: upload a PDF, place fields, send, and get back a sealed document with its audit trail that anyone can verify offline. Self-hostable, embeddable and API-first.

Pre-release: designed to support e-signature laws such as ESIGN and eIDAS; no legal or compliance claim is made (docs/09).
