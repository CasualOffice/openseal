Roadmap

Where OpenSeal stands and what comes next, generated from the project's own roadmap and tracker. Evidence integrity comes first, so the engine and server were built before the features on top.

This is the project's plan, not a commitment. No dates are promised; sizes and order can change. Ideas and requests are welcome as GitHub issues.

Done and in progress

  1. Phase D: Design

    Closed

    An agreed design, so later phases need no do-over. 6 of 6 exit criteria met.

  2. Phase 0: Foundation

    Closed

    A repository where a broken change cannot land unnoticed. 5 of 5 exit criteria met.

  3. Phase 1: PDF and evidence core

    Closed

    A headless engine that admits any PDF safely, places and flattens field values, seals at PAdES B-T to B-LTA, verifies any signed PDF, and builds an audit chain and certificate of completion. No server, no UI. 7 of 7 exit criteria met.

  4. Phase 2: Envelope engine and server

    Closed

    An API-only e-signature service: create, send, sign (through API-driven test clients), complete, seal and download, with tenancy, persistence, email and webhooks. No production UI. 6 of 6 stages built; 5 of 5 exit criteria met.

    • 2A Model and workflow engine
    • 2B Persistence, tenancy, audit immutability, blob store
    • 2C Server, auth, OpenAPI, rate limits
    • 2D Worker, job queue, email, webhooks
    • 2E keyd with PKCS#11
    • 2F Single-node Compose deployment

Release 1: the common case first

v0.0.1 published

Release 1 covers the common 60–70% of use; everything else follows as slices, in order, after it. Nothing is removed.

  • Upload PDF (and Word through the converter)
  • Place signature, initials, date, text and checkbox fields
  • Sequential and parallel signers and carbon copies
  • Email invitations and reminders
  • Decline and void
  • Email link, access code and email one-time code
  • Signing on phone and desktop
  • Sealed PDF with certificate of completion and audit trail, downloadable and verifiable
  • Simple templates
  • The sender's dashboard
  • The API and webhooks
  • Single-node Docker deployment (Phase 2F)

After Release 1

Work continues as vertical slices: thin features cut through every layer, then deepened. Grouped by theme; each card is a slice in the tracker.
