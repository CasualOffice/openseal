Brand and attribution

How to credit OpenSeal in a product built with it. Everything here is optional: the Apache-2.0 licence asks only for the notices described below.

Logos

Use the files as they are: don't recolour, stretch, crop or add effects. Leave clear space of at least a quarter of the logo's height around it.

Badge and wordmark, light backgrounds Download the light-background logo, SVG
Badge and wordmark, dark backgrounds Download the dark-background logo, SVG
Badge alone, for small spaces (at least 16 px) Download the badge, SVG

Wording

  • Preferred: Powered by OpenSeal
  • Alternative: Built with OpenSeal
  • Link: https://github.com/CasualOffice/openseal

Write "OpenSeal" as one word with a capital S. Avoid wording that suggests a partnership or endorsement, such as "Official OpenSeal partner" or "Certified by OpenSeal", unless the project has agreed to it in writing. Using OpenSeal does not mean your product is endorsed, certified, sponsored or maintained by the OpenSeal project or CasualOffice, and the OpenSeal name and logo are not to be used as your product's own.

What the licence requires

These are the conditions of the Apache License 2.0 (section 4), not project policy. When you redistribute OpenSeal or a work derived from it, in source or binary form:

  1. give recipients a copy of the LICENSE;
  2. keep the copyright, patent, trademark and attribution notices from the source you received;
  3. mark files you modified as changed;
  4. include the NOTICE file's attribution notices in a reasonable place: a NOTICE file shipped with your product, its documentation, or a third-party notices screen.

Running OpenSeal as a hosted service without distributing it does not trigger these conditions. Forks are welcome under the same terms, and may have a name of their own.

What the project asks (voluntary)

If OpenSeal helps you, please say so: in an application footer, an About dialog, a settings or acknowledgements page, your documentation, your third-party notices, or an integration listing. This is a request, not a condition; leaving it out does not affect your rights. OpenSeal never adds attribution to your application or to the PDFs it produces.

Accessibility

  • A logo used as attribution needs a text alternative: alt="Powered by OpenSeal" on the image, or visible text beside a decorative image with alt="".
  • A link must be reachable by keyboard and have a visible focus style; its accessible name should say where it goes.
  • Text should meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (4.5:1 for small text). Use the dark-background logo on dark themes.
  • Attribution should never cover content, block interaction, flash, or appear as a pop-up, banner or watermark.

Snippets

Plain text

Powered by OpenSeal - https://github.com/CasualOffice/openseal

Markdown

Powered by [OpenSeal](https://github.com/CasualOffice/openseal)

HTML

A small footer credit that follows the page's colour scheme. The image is decorative because the link text names it.

<a class="powered-by-openseal" href="https://github.com/CasualOffice/openseal">
  <picture>
    <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="/assets/openseal-logo-dark.svg">
    <img src="/assets/openseal-logo.svg" alt="" width="84" height="20">
  </picture>
  <span>Powered by OpenSeal</span>
</a>
<style>
  .powered-by-openseal { display: inline-flex; gap: .5rem; align-items: center; font-size: .875rem; color: inherit; }
  .powered-by-openseal:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid currentColor; outline-offset: 2px; }
</style>

React (TSX)

export function PoweredByOpenSeal({ dark = false }: { dark?: boolean }) {
  return (
    <a
      href="https://github.com/CasualOffice/openseal"
      style={{ display: "inline-flex", gap: "0.5rem", alignItems: "center", fontSize: "0.875rem", color: "inherit" }}
    >
      <img src={dark ? "/assets/openseal-logo-dark.svg" : "/assets/openseal-logo.svg"} alt="" width={84} height={20} />
      <span>Powered by OpenSeal</span>
    </a>
  );
}

Copy the SVGs into your own static files; the paths above are examples. From BRANDING.md and ATTRIBUTION.md.
