Electronic disclosure

Versions of the electronic records and signature disclosure recipients consent to.

Endpoints
  • GET/api/v1/ersd-versions
  • POST/api/v1/ersd-versions

List ERSD versions

GET/api/v1/ersd-versions

The account's electronic disclosure: the one the workflow policy names, with its text, and the published versions

current is the disclosure settings.workflow_policy.ersd names, with its published text (text null when no intact published row has that hash), or null when the account requires none. items are the published versions, newest first, at most 100, without their text.

AuthAPI key or OAuth access token (Authorization: Bearer), or a staff session

Permissionersd.manage · OAuth scope admin:account

Returns

200 with a ErsdVersions object.

GET/api/v1/ersd-versions

curl

curl https://sign.example.com/api/v1/ersd-versions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENSEAL_API_KEY"

Publish ERSD version

POST/api/v1/ersd-versions

Publish disclosure text as the next version and require it, in one step

Writes the next version of language (one more than its highest), its ersd.published admin event, and settings.workflow_policy.ersd = {version, hash} with its workspace.settings_changed event, in one transaction. Envelopes already sent then ask for consent to the new version. Published rows are never changed: a new text is a new version. An empty or blank text, one with a NUL, or a bad language tag is 422 CS-API-0001; a text over 100,000 bytes is 413 CS-API-0006.

AuthAPI key or OAuth access token (Authorization: Bearer), or a staff session

Permissionersd.manage · OAuth scope admin:account

Request body

Content type application/json · required

  • textstringRequired

    The disclosure recipients read and consent to; at most 100,000 bytes of UTF-8, not blank, no NUL

    at least 1 characters · at most 100000 characters

  • languagestring

    matches ^[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9-]{1,34}$ · Default en

Returns

201 Published and required with a ErsdVersion object.

Limits

  • Electronic disclosure text (POST /ersd-versions, ERSD-01); its language tag: 100,000 bytes of UTF-8, not blank, no NUL; 2 to 35 ASCII letters, digits and -, starting with a letter
POST/api/v1/ersd-versions

curl

curl -X POST https://sign.example.com/api/v1/ersd-versions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENSEAL_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
  "text": "Example text",
  "language": "en"
}'
