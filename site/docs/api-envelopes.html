Envelopes

An envelope is one agreement: its documents, the recipients who act on them in routing order, and the fields each fills. Create a draft or create and send in one call, then follow it to completed, when every document is sealed. Envelopes carry an ETag; changing a draft needs If-Match.

List envelopes

GET/api/v1/envelopes

Envelopes, sorted and filtered, cursor-paginated

The envelopes the caller may see (their own without view_all), never another account's, and never those removed from the lists (DELETE of an envelope that ended without completing) unless deleted=true asks for exactly those (the Deleted view). waiting_for=me is the Inbox: sent envelopes of this account, whoever sent them, in which the caller is, by their account email, a recipient whose turn it is and who acts through a link (not a copy, an in-person or an embedded signer); each item then has my_recipient_id. Every item has folder_id, the caller's own folder it is filed in, or null. Newest first by default. Keyset paging: a cursor belongs to the sort and order it was issued for (another is 422), and pages stay stable under inserts.

AuthAPI key or OAuth access token (Authorization: Bearer), or a staff session

Permissionenvelopes.read · OAuth scope envelopes:read

Query parameters

  • cursorstring
  • limitinteger

    minimum 1 · maximum 200 · Default 50

  • statusstring

    One status, or several separated by commas (any of them).

  • qstring

    Text the subject, or a recipient's name or email, contains (ignoring case); at most 200 characters, no control characters.

    at most 200 characters

  • sortstring

    created_at (default), updated_at (last change: the last command that changed the envelope; downloads are not changes), expires_at (never expiring after every date), subject (ignoring case) or status (lifecycle order: draft, sent, delivered, completing, completed, declined, voided, expired). Ties are broken by id.

    One of created_at, updated_at, expires_at, subject, status · Default created_at

  • orderstring

    Default desc for the times, asc for subject and status.

    One of asc, desc

  • senderstring

    A sender's user id, or me. Without view_all another sender's id answers an empty page.

  • fromstring

    date_field at or after this instant (RFC 3339).

    RFC 3339 time

  • tostring

    date_field before this instant (RFC 3339).

    RFC 3339 time

  • date_fieldstring

    Which date from and to bound: the last change (default), creation, sending or completion. An envelope without that date is outside any range.

    One of updated_at, created_at, sent_at, completed_at · Default updated_at

  • recipientstring

    Text a recipient's name or email contains, ignoring case; at most 200 characters.

    at most 200 characters

  • folderstring

    One of the caller's folders (another member's matches nothing), or none: in none of the caller's folders.

  • deletedstring

    true: only the envelopes removed from the lists, the Deleted view.

    One of true, false · Default false

  • waiting_forstring

    me: the Inbox.

    One of me

Returns

200 with a EnvelopePage object.

Create envelope

POST/api/v1/envelopes

Create a draft, or create and send in one call

AuthAPI key or OAuth access token (Authorization: Bearer), or a staff session

Permissionenvelopes.write · OAuth scope envelopes:write

Headers

  • Idempotency-Keystring

    Up to 255 printable ASCII characters; a replay within 24 hours returns the first response.

    at most 255 characters

Request body

Content type application/json · required

  • subjectstring

    at most 255 characters

  • messagestring

    at most 10000 characters

  • statusstring

    One of draft, sent · Default draft

  • documentsarray of DocumentIn
    Child attributes of documents
    • namestringRequired

      at most 255 characters

    • content_base64stringRequired

      At most 50 MiB decoded

  • recipientsarray of RecipientIn
    Child attributes of recipients
    • refstring

      Name for fields in the same request

    • typestringRequired

      One of signer, in_person_signer, approver, viewer, cc, editor, intermediary, agent, witness, seal, notary

    • namestringRequired

      at most 255 characters

    • emailstring
    • phonestring
    • routing_orderinteger

      minimum 1 · maximum 999 · Default 1

    • deliverystring

      One of email, sms, none

    • autharray of string
    • access_codestring

      With auth: [access_code]; stored as Argon2id only

    • embedded_client_user_idstring
    • time_zone_offset_minutesinteger
    • date_formatstring
    • excluded_documentsarray of string or integer

      Documents hidden from this recipient: document ids, or in a create the index of a document of the same request. A recipient sees at least one document (422 CS-WF-0004), never a field on a hidden one (422 CS-WF-0021).

    • route_delay_hoursinteger

      Delayed routing: hours routing waits, once this recipient's turn comes, before inviting them. At most 720 at send (422 CS-WF-0023); not on a carbon copy (422 CS-WF-0004).

      minimum 0 · maximum 65535 · Default 0

  • fieldsarray of FieldIn
    Child attributes of fields
    • anchorobject
      Child attributes of anchor
      • textstringRequired
      • offset_xstring

        Decimal, in units; to the right

      • offset_ystring

        Decimal, in units; downwards

      • unitsstring

        One of points, inches, millimetres, centimetres, pixels

      • ignore_if_absentboolean
      • case_sensitiveboolean
      • whole_wordboolean
      • matchingstring

        One of first, all

    • shared_witharray of string

      at most 100 items

  • remindersobject
    Child attributes of reminders
    • first_after_daysinteger

      maximum 999

    • then_every_daysinteger

      maximum 999

  • expires_in_daysinteger
  • signing_optionsSigningOptions
    Child attributes of signing_options
    • allow_reassignboolean

      Default true

    • allow_declineboolean

      Default true

    • allow_finish_laterboolean

      Default true

    • capture_locationboolean

      Record where each recipient was on every recipient action (row GEO-01, docs/32 §2): the device's location when the signer agrees, else an approximation from the request's IP address with the reason. Absent when off.

      Default false

  • seal_profilestring

    One of pades-b-t, pades-b-lt, pades-b-lta

  • text_tagsboolean

    Place a field at each {{type:recipient[:req][:label][:WxH]}} tag in the documents; the recipient is a request ref. A malformed tag refuses the request (CS-FLD-0011).

Returns

201 with a Envelope object.

Create envelope from templates

POST/api/v1/envelopes/from-templates

One envelope from one template or several (composite templates), as a draft or sent (slice 3)

Made on the server from the templates' stored documents (scanned again) through the same path as POST /envelopes, so it is checked, audited and sent the same way. Needs templates.read as well; status: sent needs envelopes.send.

AuthAPI key or OAuth access token (Authorization: Bearer), or a staff session

Permissionenvelopes.write · OAuth scope envelopes:write

Headers

  • Idempotency-Keystring

    Up to 255 printable ASCII characters; a replay within 24 hours returns the first response.

    at most 255 characters

Request body

Content type application/json · required

  • template_idsarray of stringRequired

    Templates the caller may use (another account's, or one not shared with the caller, is 422 at /template_ids/{i}); each once

    at least 1 items · at most 10 items

  • recipientsarray of object

    at most 100 items

    Child attributes of recipients
    • rolestring

      The role this person fills (422 at /recipients/{i}/role if no template has it or it is filled twice)

    • namestringRequired
    • emailstring
    • typestring

      Only for a person with no role

      One of cc, signer

  • subjectstring

    Default: the first template's email subject, else its name

  • messagestring

    Default: the first template's message

  • statusstring

    draft (default) or sent (needs envelopes.send)

    One of draft, sent

Returns

201 with a Envelope object.

Limits

  • Template folders (TSH-03) and using templates (POST /envelopes/from-templates, slice 3): templates per move; templates per envelope; people per request: 1–200; 1–10, each once; at most 100

Create sign link

POST/api/v1/envelopes/{id}/sign-link

The Inbox: a five-minute signing link for the signed-in member, as the recipient they are

For a member signed in with a session (an API key or OAuth client is 403) who is, by their account email, a recipient of this envelope whose turn it is and who acts through a link. The ceremony records the member's sign-in (account_login) as how the link was obtained and applies every authentication the sender required. 404 when the caller neither may see the envelope nor is a recipient of it; 409 CS-WF-0007 when routing has not reached them or has passed. The body is empty or {}.

AuthAPI key or OAuth access token (Authorization: Bearer), or a staff session; any signed-in caller

  • idstringRequired

    Object id (UUIDv7)

Headers

  • Idempotency-Keystring

    Up to 255 printable ASCII characters; a replay within 24 hours returns the first response.

    at most 255 characters

201 with a SigningView object.
